## Reception prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium editorial website photograph for EZQueue, an appointment and queue management app for service businesses in Thailand. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A contemporary neighborhood salon and wellness studio reception, warm off-white plaster, natural oak joinery, a subtle forest green accent wall, brushed brass details, soft daylight and lush plants. A Thai woman salon owner in a simple ivory blouse warmly welcomes an adult Thai female customer at the reception counter. Candid professional hospitality, realistic hands and anatomy, restrained and authentic, no exaggerated posing. A small tablet sits on the counter with screen facing away. Architectural photography, natural skin texture, tasteful muted colors, high-end commercial quality. No text, no logos, no floating interface, no diagrams or watermarks. Subjects centered toward right half, atmosphere spacious and calm. This is a decorative marketing image, not an image of an actual customer business.
